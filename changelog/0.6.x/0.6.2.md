# 0.6.2 — 2026-11-27

## Added

- **`attacksurface_recon_guidance`** compares operator-supplied CNAME exports with historical address statuses for offline DNS maintenance review. ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/attack-surface-mcp-server/issues/49))
- **`attacksurface_lookup_host`** marks clipped Shodan banners and offers a `bannerDetail: "full"` follow-up that retrieves the complete current observation. ([#60](https://github.com/cyanheads/attack-surface-mcp-server/issues/60))
- **`attacksurface_enumerate_subdomains`** returns a Certspotter continuation after the current window's candidates are exhausted. ([#61](https://github.com/cyanheads/attack-surface-mcp-server/issues/61))

## Changed

- **Vitest** uses its default worker pool, with its caches excluded from desktop bundles.

## Fixed

- **CMS fingerprints** omit malformed WordPress and Drupal generator versions, including WordPress.com and Drupal 10..5, while retaining adjacent numeric versions. ([#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/attack-surface-mcp-server/issues/64))
